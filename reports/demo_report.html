Task 2 — Report and Presentation

Demo only: this report uses synthetic sales data. Replace with your actual project data and rerun the notebook before submitting.

Question

Which months, regions, and products contribute most to revenue and profit?

Data

18 synthetic records across six months, three regions, and three products. Revenue = units sold × unit price. Profit = units sold × (unit price − unit cost).

Three charts

1. Monthly revenue

2. Revenue by region

3. Profit by product

Three key insights to validate

  1. Compare month-to-month revenue and investigate peaks and dips.
  2. Compare regional revenue to identify stronger and weaker areas.
  3. Compare product profit as well as revenue before making product decisions.

Two recommendations

  1. Investigate the drivers of the strongest month/region and test whether they can be repeated.
  2. Prioritize products using both profit and demand, not revenue alone.

Limitations

The data is synthetic and small. It excludes marketing costs, returns, customer-level behavior, and longer-term seasonality. Do not present these demo findings as real business conclusions.
